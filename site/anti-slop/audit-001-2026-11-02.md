# antislop audit 001 (2026-11-02)

Scope: the Minga site in `site/` at commit `7933f6b` (branch `site-github-pages`), as built and served at `http://localhost:4321/minga/`.
Mode: after. Nothing has been changed. Pick the numbers to fix.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## Evidence gathered

- Text scan of `src/` and `dist/` for em dashes, buzzwords, arrows, `cursor`, `:active`, uppercase tracking and glow.
- Playwright at 375x812 in dark and light: horizontal overflow, axe-core (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA), and controls under 44px.
- Lighthouse (mobile, measured locally on 15 page types): performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO are 100.

## Findings

### HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-03: the landing page scrolls sideways on phones.** At 375px the document is 632px wide. Hiding the comparison section brings it back to 375px, so the cause is inside `ComparisonTable` (the table is 760px wide; something in that section escapes its scroll region).
2. **R-03 / R-39: tap targets under 44px.** Copy buttons are 32px tall, and the "Text description" summaries under both diagrams and "Show edge-ai.yaml" on the examples page are shorter than 44px.
3. **R-39: missing base cursor and active states.** `cursor: pointer` is set per component, not once in the base layer, so the `<summary>` on the examples page has none. There is no `:active` style anywhere (0 rules), so buttons and links have hover but no distinct pressed state.
4. **R-02 / R-37: em dashes.** Agent-written text has one: the sample `minga deploy` output line (`created — 1/1 task(s) placed`), which quotes the CLI's real output format. The rest come from your own repo docs rendered on the site: ADR titles on the landing page and the ADR index ("Event bus — NATS/JetStream…"), plus the roadmap (64), NATS research (75), ADRs 0002 to 0004 and the Talos guide. Your call: keep them as your voice, or edit the source markdown.
5. **R-37: no dials declared.** `site/DESIGN.md` was written by agents (the ux-designer agent and me) and approved by you, not written by you, and it never sets ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION. Agent-written direction tends toward default taste. Proposed: you confirm a dial line, e.g. `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`.
6. **R-36 / R-38: unverified facts shown as fact.** The Docker Swarm and k3s cells in the comparison table were written from general knowledge and not checked against the sources they link. The `minga deploy` expected output was reconstructed from `deploy.rs`, not captured from a run. Fix: verify both, or label them on the page ("from the CLI source, not a captured run").
7. **R-23: identity assumed without confirmation.** The maintainer name "Cristhian Ferrufino" (from git config) is used as the blog byline and as the BlogPosting JSON-LD author.
8. **R-33: HTML string patch.** `src/pages/changelog.astro` adds the "not in a tagged release" pill with a regex replace on rendered HTML. It should be a rehype step in the markdown pipeline, so a changed heading can't silently drop it.

### MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

9. **R-01 / R-07: radial glow backgrounds without a written reason.** Magenta and cyan radial gradients sit behind the hero and the final CTA. Either write the reason (echoing the Dev-Ferru cover lighting) into DESIGN.md or remove them.
10. **R-13: glow on three element types.** The focus ring, the primary button hover and the feature card hover all glow (cap is 1-2). Proposed: keep the focus ring and the primary button, drop the card hover glow.
11. **R-14: identical cards.** The 8 feature cards and the 3 problem cards share one size, icon slot and padding, with no hierarchy. Proposed: lead with the two strongest claims ("Speaks the Swarm API", "Fails loudly by default") at full width, and render the rest as a compact list.
12. **R-08: decorative arrows.** "Read more →" on every feature card, an arrow on the primary CTA and on inline section links. The prev/next pagers are directional and stay.
13. **R-06: uppercase wide-tracked labels.** Footer column headings and docs sidebar group labels use uppercase with 0.08em tracking and no written reason. (Monospace headings are kept: you chose the industrial/terminal tone, and DESIGN.md §4.2 records the reason.)
14. **R-04: one irrelevant icon.** "Rust on the hot path" uses a cog/star glyph that says nothing about Rust or the hot path. The other seven are drawn to their content, but their relevance isn't written down.

### LOW (Quality Locks)

15. **R-05: uniform section rhythm.** Every landing section has the same top border, the same 64/96px padding and a left-aligned h2. The problem section is a 3-card grid and the footer is a 4-column grid. No RHYTHM dial justifies the uniformity (see 5). The hero code block is a terminal-style window, which R-05 lists, but your brief asked for it explicitly, so it is recorded as an owner override, not a finding.
16. **R-15: generic CTA.** "Read more" on the feature cards. The final CTA labels ("Try it now", "Read the docs", "Star on GitHub", "Join the community") came from your brief and stay. Proposed card links: name the destination ("Open the quickstart", "Read ADR 0004").
17. **R-29: four hues.** Cyan, magenta, green and rose plus neutrals. Green and rose mark yes/no and limits, but the reason isn't written down. Proposed: record "green and rose are status colors, not palette" in DESIGN.md, or fold rose into magenta.
